# PulseTwin — Product Vision

## Product
PulseTwin is an industrial digital twin and operational intelligence platform for mixed-model vehicle assembly environments.

It combines machine telemetry, PLC/OT signals, manual inspections, production events, environmental conditions, quality outcomes, equipment state, inferred state, predictive models, and simulation.

The objective is to help plant teams answer:
1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. What is likely to happen next?
4. What can we safely change?
5. What would happen if we changed it?

## Product Loop
OBSERVE → UNDERSTAND → PREDICT → SIMULATE → DECIDE → VERIFY

## Principles
- Spatial before abstract.
- Evidence before prediction.
- Confidence is visible.
- Never pretend certainty.
- Safety before autonomy.
- Human operators remain accountable.
- Progressive complexity.
- Trust is earned.

## Design Ambition
PulseTwin must feel like a next-generation industrial operating environment, not a generic AI dashboard. Avoid chatbot-first layouts, generic SaaS cards, excessive gradients, glassmorphism, decorative AI effects, and unnecessary animation.

The interface should feel precise, spatial, industrial, sophisticated, calm, fast, and trustworthy.

## Prototype Objective
A user should be able to enter a 3D factory, observe production flow, identify an emerging bottleneck, inspect evidence, trace an upstream issue, create a simulation scenario, compare outcomes, and receive a recommendation.
