# Problem and Opportunity

## Factory Reality
Assembly plants mix modern connected equipment, legacy PLCs, limited telemetry, manual inspections, disconnected quality systems, and equipment from different vendors.

A useful digital twin must work with incomplete instrumentation.

## Core Problems
### Dynamic bottlenecks
Causes may include machine degradation, cycle-time variation, material quality, operator variation, upstream starvation, downstream blockage, changeovers, and environmental conditions.

### Delayed quality detection
A defect introduced early may only be detected many stations later.

### Root-cause ambiguity
Correlation is not causation. The system must distinguish evidence, hypothesis, and validated causal relationships.

### Sensor gaps
Stations may have rich telemetry, partial telemetry, manual inspection only, or almost no direct sensing.

## Opportunity
PulseTwin creates a common operational model across imperfect data sources. The value comes from combining imperfect signals into a coherent representation of production state.
