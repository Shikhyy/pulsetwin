# Demo Scenarios

## Scenario 1 — Emerging Bottleneck
Station 18 develops gradual cycle-time drift. Queue growth appears downstream. PulseTwin predicts a bottleneck before it becomes the dominant constraint.

## Scenario 2 — Early Quality Risk
A torque process begins drifting at Station 12. The final inspection failure appears later. PulseTwin traces the affected units and identifies the earlier signal.

## Scenario 3 — Sensor-Poor Station
A station has limited direct telemetry but manual checks and downstream effects. PulseTwin represents uncertainty and uses indirect evidence rather than pretending the station is fully observed.

## Scenario 4 — What-If
Station 18 cycle time increases by 8%. Simulation shows queue propagation and throughput impact.

## Scenario 5 — Maintenance Decision
Compare running with degraded equipment versus a simulated maintenance intervention.

## Demo Story
Prefer one coherent narrative that demonstrates:
observe → investigate → predict → simulate → decide → verify.
