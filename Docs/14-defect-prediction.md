# Defect Prediction

## Objective
Detect risk before a downstream inspection catches it.

## Prediction Unit
A prediction may be associated with a production unit, station, process step, or quality characteristic.

## Inputs
Combine process telemetry, production context, upstream observations, manual checks, and historical outcomes.

## Outputs
- risk probability
- predicted inspection outcome
- horizon
- evidence
- confidence
- model version

## Validation
Evaluate against later inspection outcomes. Measure false alarms and missed defects. Track performance by station and production mix.
