# Development Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Repository, architecture, design system, app shell, contracts.

## Phase 1 — Digital Factory
3D factory, 30–50 stations, production units, selection, simulated live state.

## Phase 2 — Operational Twin
Telemetry, station state, events, data quality, digital thread.

## Phase 3 — Prediction
Anomaly detection, bottleneck prediction, quality prediction, evidence.

## Phase 4 — Investigation
Root-cause exploration, history, timeline, evidence graph.

## Phase 5 — Simulation
Scenario creation, discrete-event simulation, comparison, recommendations.

## Phase 6 — OT
Simulated PLC, OPC UA architecture, read-only integration, control gateway.

## Phase 7 — Validation
Historical replay, synthetic defect injection, model evaluation, workflow testing.

## Phase 8 — Demo
One polished end-to-end scenario rather than disconnected feature demos.
