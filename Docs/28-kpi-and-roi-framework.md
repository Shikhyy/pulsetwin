# KPI and ROI Framework

## Operational KPIs
- throughput
- OEE
- cycle time
- downtime
- first-pass yield
- defect rate
- scrap
- rework
- bottleneck duration

## Predictive KPIs
- precision
- false alarms
- false negatives
- lead time
- prevented defects
- calibration

## Business Impact
Estimate:
Defects prevented × cost per defect
Downtime prevented × cost per minute
Throughput improvement × contribution margin

## Leadership View
Leadership sees:
Current performance → Opportunity → Expected impact → Investment → Confidence → Rollout recommendation.
